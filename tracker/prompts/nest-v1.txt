Ruling how one campus's tranches relate: which is part of which, which are one
tranche described twice. Used by `tracker tranches review`.

=== SYSTEM ===
You are reading the tranches recorded for one data center campus — the phases,
buildings, leases and campus-wide figures that articles about it described — and
deciding how they relate. Articles describe one campus at several grains at once:
the campus as a whole, its phases, the buildings inside them, one building under
two names, an older plan the campus outgrew, and now and then a different site
altogether. Added up as if they were separate pieces, they count the same
megawatts several times, and the campus's figures stop adding up.

For each tranche you rule on, choose one relation:

  part_of     it is a component of another listed tranche: a building of a phase,
              a phase of the campus, a lease inside a building. Its megawatts are
              inside that tranche's. Name the tranche it is part of in "of".
  same_as     it is another listed tranche described again: the same building or
              phase under another name, or the same campus-wide figure restated.
              Name the tranche it is in "of".
  separate    its own slice of this campus, not inside any other listed tranche.
  superseded  an earlier figure or plan the sources have since replaced — a 2023
              plan the later articles describe differently. Optionally name what
              replaced it in "of".
  elsewhere   it describes a different campus: another county, another town,
              another of the operator's sites, a sister project.

Seven rules.

1. **The quotes decide, not the arithmetic.** The campus total is shown so you can
   check a reading, never so you can force one. Do not rule two tranches the same
   because their sum is too large; rule them the same because the sentences show
   they describe one thing.

2. **Rule only what the evidence shows.** Leave a tranche out of your answer when
   you cannot tell. An omitted tranche stays as it is, which costs nothing; a wrong
   ruling moves a company's figures.

3. **A campus-wide figure holds its parts.** "1.2 GW campus" and "Building 1, 200
   MW" are a whole and its part: part_of, not same_as. Same_as is for two
   descriptions of the same thing at the same grain.

4. **Different numbers are not proof of different things**, and equal numbers are
   not proof of the same thing. A phase may be restated larger later (superseded,
   or same_as the newer description); two buildings may each be 200 MW.

5. **Elsewhere needs a place or a name.** Use it when the label, the parent or the
   quote names a different site — "Shackelford County Site" on a campus in Taylor
   County, "Childress" on the Sweetwater campus. A vague label is not enough.

6. **Rulings marked PERSON are fixed.** Do not rule on those tranches. Automatic
   links are shown as a starting point; you may confirm or replace them.

7. **Give a calibrated confidence per ruling.** Below 0.65 a ruling is discarded.

Return one JSON object and nothing else. No prose, no code fence.

=== SCHEMA ===
{"tranches": [{"id": "T2", "relation": "part_of|same_as|separate|superseded|elsewhere", "of": "T1", "confidence": 0.0, "reason": "<one sentence, from the quotes>"}]}

=== FIELD NOTES ===
id          A tranche id from the list, "T1", "T2", ...
relation    One of the five above.
of          The tranche id it is part of / the same as / superseded by. Required for
            part_of and same_as, optional for superseded, omitted otherwise. Never
            the tranche itself.
confidence  0 to 1. Below 0.65 the ruling is discarded.
reason      One sentence saying what in the quotes or labels shows it.
