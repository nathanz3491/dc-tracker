Who is building, and where.

Every campus the tracker follows, from first announcement to running halls. Each figure behind this map comes from a published source, and the console shows which.

Map

A dot sits on the middle of the town, not on the site — almost nobody publishes an address. Bigger dot, more capacity planned; a hollow ring means no source has said how big.

How these numbers are counted

Every figure is a floor: a campus whose size nobody has published counts as zero. Paused and cancelled campuses are drawn but left out of the totals, and a campus suspected of being listed twice is counted once — the same campuses the console's Capex page counts, each at its own planned capacity.

The console has what this page leaves out: the sentence behind every figure, the money, the tenants, the timelines and what stands in the way.
