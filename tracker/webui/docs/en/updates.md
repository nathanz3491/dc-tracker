# Updates

**Updates** is what was reported, recently, about what you watch. It is the page to open first each day, and the page the morning email is a summary of.

What it covers is decided by your watchlist on [Monitor](monitor). If you watch nothing yet, the page says so and links there.

## What counts as an update

An update is something a source reported about a campus you watch: a milestone (land bought, a permit approved, ground broken, power switched on, a first customer), a delay, an expansion, or an obstacle (opposition, a grid limit, a lawsuit, a water dispute, financing trouble).

Only what was **reported in the last two months** is shown. "Reported" means the date the article was published; if that is not known, the date of the event itself; failing both, the day the tracker stored it.

Whether an update is good or bad comes from what kind of event it is — power switched on is good, a delay is bad — not from a model's opinion.

## The chips at the top

One chip for each entry on your watchlist, showing:

- the entry's name and how many projects it matches (**Np**);
- **▼n** bad updates (red) and **▲n** good ones (green) in the window;
- **no match** (amber) if nothing in the tracker matches the entry yet.

Click a chip to show only that entry's updates; **show all watches** clears it. **Edit the list on Monitor →** takes you to your watchlist.

## The four tiles

| Tile | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **New since your last email** | Updates that reached the tracker after your last morning email. Before your first email it reads **New in the last day**. |
| **Needs attention** | Bad updates: obstacles and slips. Click to filter the list to them. |
| **Good news** | Good updates. Click to filter. |
| **Still open** | Obstacles reported in the last two months that are still open, and on how many of your projects. Click to go to Monitor. |

## Controls

- **week / month / 2 months** — how far back the list goes. The default is a week.
- **all / needs attention / good news / worth emailing** — which updates to show. *Worth emailing* is the bar an update must clear to be put in the morning email.
- **last crawl** on the right — when the tracker last fetched new articles. It turns amber, with how many days ago, if that was more than 48 hours ago: the news may be behind.

## The list

**New since your last email** comes first: what arrived after your last email (or in the last 24 hours, if you have not had one yet).

**Reported this week** (or this month, or the last two months) follows, grouped by the day it was reported — *Today*, *Yesterday*, then dates — with each day's count of updates, bad, good and emailed.

Each update is a card:

- a coloured edge and sign: **+** green for good, **−** red for bad, **·** grey for neutral;
- the headline, then the campus as *Company — Project*, which opens [its page](project-page);
- flags where they apply:
  - **expected, not reached** — the date is in the future, so this is a schedule, not something that has happened;
  - **the blocker moved** — the campus's biggest obstacle changed;
  - **✓ emailed** — it was in one of your morning emails;
  - **worth emailing** — it clears the email's bar;
- what it changes, the detail, and the sentence it rests on;
- when it was reported, when it happened (or is due), the publisher, and which of your watch entries it matched. **+N more reports** means other articles said the same thing.

**By company** sums the window up per watch entry: projects, how many need attention, good news, open blockers, and where each stands (for example *power: waiting on energized*). Click a name to filter the list to it.

At the bottom, **Show N unconfirmed — nobody could quote them** reveals updates that were reported but could not be backed by a sentence from the article. They are shown faded and are never emailed. See [Where a value came from](provenance).

## The morning email

At **8:00 China Standard Time** each day the tracker emails you what is new and worth emailing on your watchlist, if you have not already been sent it.

- **On a day with news** the subject names the first update and how many more there are. The email lists each one with when it happened, when it was reported and a link to the publisher, then a short *watch for* list of what would move your projects next, and a button to open this week on the Updates page.
- **On a quiet day** the subject says there are no new updates and how many of your projects still have open blockers; the email lists what is still open and what would clear it.

An update goes in the email if you have not been sent it before, it clears the *worth emailing* bar, it was reported in the last 60 days, and either it reached the tracker since your last email or it was reported in the last 14 days.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> There is no unsubscribe link. The email follows your watchlist: an **empty watchlist** stops it, and so does **watch everything** — watching the whole database is not mailed, because it would be the whole database. Change either on [Monitor](monitor).

If you missed an email, everything it would have contained is still on this page for two months.

## When the page is empty

- *Nothing reported this week* — nothing on your list was reported in this window. Widen the window, add a company on Monitor, or look at what is still open there.
- *Nothing for X this week* — the chip you picked had no news, but others did. Click the chip again to see everything.
- *Nothing matches this filter* — choose **all**.
