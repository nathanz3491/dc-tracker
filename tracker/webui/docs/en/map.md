# Map

**Map** puts every campus that has a location on a map of the United States.

> [!WARNING]
> A dot sits on the **centre of the town or county** the campus is filed under, not on the site itself. Two campuses in one town share a point, and a campus outside town appears in it. The map is for seeing where activity is, not for finding a site.

The page says how many campuses have coordinates; the rest are still in the list beside the map.

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Flat / Relief** | A flat map, or a 3D view with columns. Relief is offered only where the browser supports it. |
| **colour** | Colour dots by **phase**, **confidence** or **operator**. |
| **state fill** | Shade states by **project count**, **cited MW**, or not at all (flat map only). |
| **Operator clusters** | Draw a dashed ring around each operator's sites, labelled with its name and how many (flat map only). |
| **− / zoom / +** | Zoom from 1× to 12× (flat map only). |
| **Reset view** | Back to the whole country. |

## The flat map

- A dot's **area grows with planned capacity**. A **hollow dashed ring** means no capacity has been cited for that campus — it is not small, it is unknown.
- A **pulsing red halo** marks a campus with an open obstacle severe enough to stop work.
- Zoom with the wheel, by pinching, or with the buttons, and drag to move. Dots keep their size as you zoom.
- Hover a state to see how many campuses and cited megawatts it holds. Hover a dot to see the campus, place and capacity; click it to open [its page](project-page).
- The legend explains the phase or confidence colours. With **operator** colouring there is no legend, and only five colours repeat, so two operators can share a colour.

## Relief

Columns rise with planned capacity; a short faint pin marks a campus with no cited capacity. States are shaded by how many campuses they hold. The view turns slowly for a few seconds; drag to rotate and scroll to zoom. In Relief, **operator** colouring falls back to phase colours.

## The list

Beside the map, every campus sorted by planned capacity — including those without coordinates — with its place, phase and whether something is blocking it. Click one to open it.
