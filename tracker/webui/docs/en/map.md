# Map

**Map** puts every campus that has a location on a map of the United States, with the national and state totals above and below it.

> [!WARNING]
> A dot sits on the **centre of the town or county** the campus is filed under, not on the site itself. Two campuses in one town share a point, and a campus outside town appears in it. The map is for seeing where activity is, not for finding a site.

Under the map, the page says how many campuses have a location and are drawn. A campus without one is still in [Projects](projects) and still counted in the figures.

## The figures

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Campuses tracked** | The campuses the [Capex](capex) page counts: paused and cancelled ones are left out, and a campus suspected of being listed twice is counted once. |
| **Capacity planned** | Those campuses' planned capacity, each at the figure for the campus as a whole. |
| **Capacity operating** | The part of it already built and running. |
| **States** | How many states those campuses are in. |

A campus whose size nobody has published counts as zero, so both capacities are floors. The Capex page's buyer rows can add up to slightly more: where a campus's buildings are listed by buyer, the buildings on a few campuses sum past the campus's own figure, and the map takes the campus's.

## Reading the map

- **Colour is stage**: announced, permitting, construction, operational, paused or cancelled. Paused and cancelled campuses are drawn, but not counted in the figures.
- **Size is planned capacity**, in five steps: under 100 MW, 100–299 MW, 300–999 MW, 1–3 GW, and 3 GW or more. The legend under the map shows each one.
- A **hollow dashed ring** means nobody has said how big the campus is. It is not small; it is unknown.
- A **state's shade** deepens with the capacity planned in it, from the colour of the land to amber.
- A campus suspected of being listed twice is drawn once.

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Stage buttons** | Each shows a stage's colour and how many dots it has. Click one to hide that stage's dots; click again to bring them back. |
| **− / +** | Zoom out or in, between 1× and 10×. The figure between them is the current zoom. |
| **Reset** | Back to the whole country. |

You can also zoom with the wheel or by pinching, and drag to move. Dots keep their size as you zoom, so a crowded area such as Northern Virginia comes apart.

**Hover a dot** to see the campus, its company and place, its stage and planned capacity, and whether an obstacle is blocking it. **Click it** to open [its page](project-page). **Hover a state** to see how many campuses it has and the capacity planned there.

## By state

Below the map, a table lists every state with a counted campus, the largest planned capacity first, with how many campuses each has. It counts the same way as the figures above it.
