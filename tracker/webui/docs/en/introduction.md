# What dc-tracker is

dc-tracker follows data-center campuses being built across the United States: how much capacity is planned and running, the money behind it, who it is for, and what could hold it up. Every figure shows where it came from, down to the sentence where there is one.

## What you can do with it

- **Follow the news on what you care about.** Put companies, tenants or single campuses on your watchlist, and the [Updates](updates) page and a morning email tell you what was reported about them. See [Monitor](monitor).
- **Look up a campus.** The [Projects](projects) table holds every campus the tracker knows, and each has [its own page](project-page) with its figures, the sources behind them, its tranches, its obstacles and a timeline.
- **See who is paying.** [Capex](capex) adds the capacity and investment up by buyer: the company that will actually use the buildings, not only the one constructing them.
- **Check a figure for yourself.** Hover any value to see the sentence it rests on; open [Sources](sources) to see which publishers the whole dataset leans on.

## The one rule behind it

What a model reads out of an article is not automatically a fact. Articles are read by a language model, and every value it extracts must come with a sentence that is really in the article and really states that value. Values that pass are shown as quoted; values that do not are kept, but marked **待确认** ("to be confirmed") and left out of anything the tracker vouches for. [Where a value came from](provenance) explains the labels you will see.

> [!TIP]
> If you only read one page of these docs, read [Where a value came from](provenance). Every number in the console carries one of its labels, and they change how far you should trust it.

## What it covers, and what it does not

| Covers | Does not cover |
| --- | --- |
| Data-center campuses in the US, from first announcement to operation | Sites outside the US |
| Planned and running capacity in megawatts, investment, dates, tenant, phase | Rents, prices, contract terms |
| Obstacles reported in the news: power, permits, opposition, water, supply, money | Private information; everything comes from public sources |
| Milestones: land, permits, power, construction, first customer | A forecast of its own; the tracker reports what sources say |

The data is gathered from public sources: news and trade press, operators' own announcements, government documents and company filings. [Where the data comes from](methodology) describes them, and [How the data is kept current](how-it-is-kept-current) describes the daily update.
