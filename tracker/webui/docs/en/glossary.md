# Glossary

Terms you will meet in the console and these docs, alphabetically.

**Approximate (~)** — a figure the source hedged with "about", "roughly" or "an estimated". See [Numbers and dates](numbers-and-dates).

**At least (+)** — a figure the source gave as a floor: "more than", "over". Shown with a trailing **+**.

**Biggest obstacle** — the most serious open obstacle on a campus, in one sentence. See [Phases and progress](progress).

**Blocking** — the most severe obstacle level: work has stopped.

**Buyer** — the company that will use a campus's capacity, which may differ from the one building it. [Capex](capex) totals by buyer.

**Campus** — one row of the tracker: one operator's site in one place. It may hold several buildings.

**Citation** — a source attached to a campus, with the values it supports.

**Company** — one record per company: its one name, what kind of company it is, its parent, and every spelling folded into it. See [Company pages](companies).

**Confidence** — a score from 0 to 3 for how well backed a campus is. See [Confidence](confidence).

**Default** — a value shown because nobody stated one and the field cannot be empty; in practice a phase of *announced*. See [Where a value came from](provenance).

**Delay risk** — on a [company page](companies) and a [project's page](project-page), how likely a campus is to miss its date, read from evidence on its page: *high* for a passed date with nothing running, two or more slips, or an obstacle that stopped work; *elevated* for one slip, an obstacle that moved the schedule, or a date within a year with no construction reported; otherwise *low*. A reading, not a forecast.

**Derived** — a value looked up or worked out rather than reported: Census geography, compute from megawatts.

**Duplicate** — one campus held in two rows. See [Duplicates](duplicates).

**Energized** — power is connected and on.

**Facility power (◒)** — the whole site's power draw, including cooling; usually 10–30% above IT load.

**Floor** — a figure that is a minimum. Every total on [Capex](capex) is a floor.

**Forecast** — a milestone that is only expected. Never counted as reached.

**Former** — a party whose role on a campus has ended: a tenant whose lease ended, an owner that sold. Kept on the campus's page; nobody's buyer now.

**GW** — gigawatt: 1,000 megawatts.

**H200-equivalent** — capacity restated as NVIDIA H200 accelerators, about 770 per megawatt.

**Held** — a relationship between the figures the tracker enforces on every write; a write that would break one is refused. See [How the figures relate](invariants).

**Hyperscaler** — a large cloud company building or leasing capacity for its own use.

**Implied** — a milestone deduced from a later one (no groundbreaking without land), not reported. Power is never implied.

**Inferred** — a model's judgement from stored facts, used only for likely obstacles and signals to watch for.

**IT load** — the power the computing equipment itself uses. What planned capacity is meant to measure.

**Landlord** — a developer that builds data centers to lease to others.

**Material** — the middle obstacle level: a source says the schedule or scope moved.

**Merge** — folding duplicate rows into one. Cannot be undone.

**MW** — megawatt, a measure of electrical capacity. All capacity columns are in MW.

**Nameplate (⚡)** — a power plant's rated output; not a data-center load.

**Neocloud** — a company renting out GPU computing; counted as an end user, like a hyperscaler.

**n/a** — empty because the field cannot apply yet, such as running capacity before anything is built.

**No named customer** — capacity with no identifiable buyer, shown last on [Capex](capex).

**Obstacle** — something reported as standing in a campus's way: grid capacity, permits, opposition, water, supply, financing.

**Operator** — the company building and running a campus.

**Overdue** — on a company page's supply chart, capacity whose campus is a suspected delay: still expected, with no date anyone now stands behind.

**Party** — a company involved in a campus, with its role: developer, owner, operator, customer, utility or contractor.

**Phase** — how far along a campus is: announced, permitting, construction, operational, paused, cancelled.

**Precision** — how precisely a date was stated: day, month, quarter, half or year.

**PUE** — power usage effectiveness: facility power divided by IT load. Modern large sites are about 1.1–1.3.

**Quoted** — a value backed by a sentence the tracker checked is in the article and states it.

**Relationship not yet closed** — a relationship between a campus's figures that its sources do not yet settle, such as tranches that describe it twice. Listed on its page; changes no figure. See [How the figures relate](invariants).

**Ruled out** — a pair of rows judged to be different sites; or a claim judged not to describe this campus.

**Scope** — what a figure covers: this site, one building, a programme, a region or a portfolio.

**Serving** — a building or tranche with a customer running on it.

**Shell complete** — a building that is up but has no power yet.

**Signal to watch for** — the next milestone on a track: the news that would show a campus moving.

**Statement** — one thing one source says about a campus, a tranche, a party, a milestone or an obstacle, with how far it can be trusted and the sentence behind it. Every figure is chosen from them. See [Where a value came from](provenance).

**Superseded** — a claim that was correct when published but has since been restated.

**Subsidiary** — a company recorded as part of another, its parent. Its campuses and figures stay its own.

**Supply** — on a company page, the capacity it is credited with that is not yet running, by the year each campus is due, over this year and the next two.

**Suspected delay** — a campus whose expected online date has passed, for the whole month, quarter or year it names, with nothing reported since saying it runs — and a search for news of it going live, made after the date passed, found none. See [Numbers and dates](numbers-and-dates).

**Tertiary source** — a directory, wiki or digest. Fills gaps only.

**Tier (T0–T3)** — the tracker's ladder of row quality: sourced, sound, complete, settled. See [Quality tiers](tiers).

**Track** — one of five independent progress ladders: site control, permits, power, construction, customer & finance.

**Tranche** — one building, phase or hall of a campus, with its own capacity and state. Tranches nest: a building can be part of a phase, a phase part of the campus, and the campus counts each megawatt once. One that is another name for a tranche, superseded, or another campus's is *set aside* and counted nowhere.

**待确认** — "to be confirmed": a value read from an article but not backed by a checked sentence.

**Watchlist** — the companies and campuses you follow; decides your [Updates](updates) and morning email.
