# Known limitations

What the tracker cannot do, or does imperfectly. Each one changes how far you should rely on something, so they are stated plainly.

## About the evidence

- **Many values are not yet quote-backed.** A large share of stored values are 待确认: read from an article, but not backed by a checked sentence. They are labelled, and left out of confidence, but they are in the table. Use **Quoted only** in [Projects](projects) when that matters.
- **Promotional figures pass straight through.** A developer's own announcement can be quoted exactly and still be optimistic. Nothing is checked against an outside record such as a utility filing.
- **Syndicated stories count more than once.** The same press release reprinted by several outlets looks like several independent sources, which can raise [confidence](confidence) without real corroboration.
- **A person's sign-off never expires.** A campus checked by a person keeps a confidence of 3 even as the news moves on.

## About figures and dates

- **Most capacity figures do not say which kind of megawatt they are.** Unmarked usually means "not stated", not "IT load". See [Numbers and dates](numbers-and-dates).
- **Many dates are year-only.** A large share of expected online dates were given only as a year. Read [Capex](capex)'s quarter view as a shape.
- **Ties are broken by when an article was read.** When two equally credible sources disagree, the more recently *fetched* wins, which is not always the more recently *published*.
- **Severity says little.** Sources rarely say an obstacle moved the schedule, so nearly every obstacle is *watch*.

## About coverage

- **Power-side sources are thin.** The tracker reads little from utilities and grid operators, so transmission and grid obstacles are under-recorded.
- **Some structured public records are not read yet,** such as certain counties' own data-center listings.
- **Two major trade publishers block automated reading.** Their stories reach the tracker only when another outlet or the operator carries the same news. See [Where the data comes from](methodology).
- **Private developers publish less.** Capacity and investment are often never stated for them, so those fields stay empty. See [Why a field is empty](empty-fields).

## About places

- **Map points are town centres, not sites.** See [Map](map).
- **Town and county rows are never merged automatically.** The same site filed once by town and once by county stays as two rows until a person or the nightly update settles it. See [Duplicates](duplicates).

## About the console

- **Merges cannot be undone,** and a merged row's address stops working.
- **The AI panels are a model's reading.** They can be wrong, and nothing they say is stored or counts as evidence.
- **The Capex banner says to "fold" duplicates,** but folding is done by whoever maintains the tracker, not from the console.
- **The console's interface is in English.** These docs are in English and Chinese.
