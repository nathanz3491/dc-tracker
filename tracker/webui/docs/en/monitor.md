# Monitor

**Monitor** is where you choose what to follow, and where you see what is still standing in the way of everything you follow.

## Your watchlist

The top half is your list. It decides what [Updates](updates) shows you and what the morning email contains. It is yours alone: other accounts have their own.

An entry is one of three kinds:

| Kind | How it matches | How to add it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **company** | Every campus where that company is the operator, developer or tenant. | Type the name and pick it. |
| **project name** | Campuses whose name matches the words you typed. Loose, by name. | Type a project's name and press Enter. |
| **this project** | Exactly one campus, by its number, whatever it is later renamed. | Press ☆ on the campus (see below). |

Each row shows its kind, the name (a link to the campus, or to the [company's page](companies) when it covers several), any note, how many projects it matches — or **no match yet** in amber — and the day you added it. **✕** removes it.

### Adding an entry

Type into **watch a company, a tenant, or one project…**. With the box empty, the list suggests the operators and tenants with the most projects.

- Each suggestion says what it is (**operator**, **tenant** or **project**) and how many projects or megawatts it covers. What you already watch is dimmed and cannot be picked twice.
- Every word you type must match. Projects are matched on name, company, tenant, city, county and state.
- If nothing matches, **Enter** watches the name anyway. It starts reporting as soon as a matching project appears.
- Keys: **↑ / ↓** move through the list, **Enter** picks, **Esc** closes it.

Tenants listed only as "undisclosed", "unnamed" or "confidential" are not offered: they are not a company you can follow.

### Watching one project: ☆

The ☆ button appears next to every row of [Projects](projects), and as a wide button on a [project's page](project-page).

- **☆ Watch** — press to watch this campus by its number.
- **★ Watching** — you watch it; press to stop.
- **★ Watching via …** (dimmed) — a company entry, or *watch everything*, already covers it. Pressing it takes you here, to change that entry; it does not unwatch.

### Watch everything

**watch everything** switches your Updates page to every campus in the tracker; **watch only my list** switches back. While it is on, **the morning email is not sent**: it would be the whole database every day.

## What is still in the way

The bottom half looks at every project you follow and asks what is blocking it.

### The tiles

| Tile | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Projects followed** | How many campuses your list covers. |
| **Blocked** | How many have an obstacle reported in the last two months. |
| **Blocking or material** | Obstacles by severity: *blocking* (work has stopped), *material* (a source says the schedule or scope moved), *to watch* (raised, no stated effect). |
| **Unconfirmed** | Obstacles that were reported but could not be backed by a quote. |

Obstacles nobody has reported for two months are left out here, and the page says how many. They are still on each project's own page.

### Filters

- **every blocker / material and worse / blocking only** — the lowest severity to show.
- **filter by company or project…** — narrow the cards.

### A project card

- The company (a link to its [page](companies)), the place, and the project's name (a link to [its page](project-page)).
- **N open**, coloured by the worst severity, or **nothing open**.
- Five cells, one per progress track — site, permits, power, construction, customer & finance — each reading *done*, *blocked*, *nothing yet* or where it stands. See [Phases and progress](progress).
- Each open blocker: its severity and kind, which track it holds up, how long it has been open, a summary, the publisher (opens the article in a new tab), and **the quote** to show the sentence.
- A signpost: **Would clear it:** the milestone that would unblock the track, or **Next step:** the next milestone, followed by what to look for in the news.

At the bottom, **Show N projects with nothing open** lists the rest, and **Show N unconfirmed** reveals obstacles nobody could quote, faded.
