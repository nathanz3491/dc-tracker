# Why a field is empty

An empty cell is not always a gap. Often it is the correct answer, and the tracker distinguishes the cases.

## Empty because it does not apply — n/a

Some fields cannot have a value yet. **Running capacity** on a campus that is announced, in permitting or under construction is {{v:na|—}} *n/a*: nothing is switched on, so there is nothing to report. These are drawn fainter than other blanks so they read as "nothing to see" rather than "nothing found".

## Empty because there is nothing — usually right

- **Buyer.** A large cloud company building its own campus has no outside customer. An empty buyer on such a campus is correct.
- **Biggest obstacle.** Empty means no open obstacle has been reported. Most campuses have none at any moment.
- **County.** A city that spans several counties — Houston, Austin, Kansas City — gets no county rather than a guessed one.

## Empty because it was searched for and not published

The tracker goes looking for missing figures: it searches for and reads further articles about a campus. When a field has been **searched for twice and nothing has been published**, it stops spending effort on it and records it as *published nowhere*. The field stays empty — no figure is invented — and it reopens automatically as soon as the campus gains a new source.

This is common for private developers, who often never publish capacity or investment, and for dates nobody has committed to.

## Empty because the only figure was ruled out

A field can lose its value when the only figure behind it turns out to be wrong for this campus: a figure for a whole programme, a different site, or an early plan the campus has outgrown. The tracker would rather show nothing than a figure it knows is about something else. A [project's page](project-page) shows such decisions in its notes, and **N other claims** shows the figure that was set aside.

## Empty because nobody has found it yet

And sometimes a figure simply has not been reported in anything the tracker has read. These are what the nightly update works on — see [How the data is kept current](how-it-is-kept-current).

## Not empty, but not a fact

Two things look filled but carry little weight:

- a {{v:defaulted|announced}} **default** phase: nobody stated one;
- a {{v:unconfirmed|1,200 MW}} **待确认** value: read out of an article but not backed by a sentence.

See [Where a value came from](provenance).
